    <div align="left"><h2>Introduction</h2><br />
Hello, since none else did a Tutorial of this mission, I am going to do it. <br />
<br />
<h2>Requirements:</h2><br />
-<a href='http://www.hackthissite.org/missions/basic/'>Basic</a> challenges completion <br />
-HTML moderate knowledge<br />
-A web browser with an inspecting tool<br />
<br />
<h3>Ok. Now let&amp;#039;s take a look at the challenge.</h3><br />
Message:<i>&quot;Hey man, I need a big favour from you. Remember that website I showed you once before? Uncle Arnold&amp;#039;s Band Review Page? Well, a long time ago I made a $500 bet with a friend that my band would be at the top of the list by the end of the year. Well, as you already know, two of my band members have died in a horrendous car accident... but this ass hole still insists that the bet is on!<br />
I know you&amp;#039;re good with computers and stuff, so I was wondering, is there any way for you to hack this website and make my band on the top of the list? My band is Raging Inferno. Thanks a lot, man!&quot;</i><br />
<br />
We take note: he wants that his band comes on the top of the list.<br />
<br />
If we go on that page, we see that the bands are ordered from the best rated (top of the page) to the worst rated (end of the page). Also, we see that we can give a rate from 1 to 5. <br />
That means the first one is more overrated than normal (so don&amp;#039;t feel guilty in doing this :P).<br />
How can we move the customer&amp;#039;s band to the top of the page? I&amp;#039;ll let you think.<br />
<br />
This challenge is very easy, but if you&amp;#039;re stuck:<br />
<br /> <b>spoiler:</b> <br /><div class="spoiler">Give your customer an enourmous rate.</div> <br />
<br />
<br />
Thanks to all the HackThisSite team, they made a great job!<br />
